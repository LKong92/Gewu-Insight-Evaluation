# Release scope

The release contains 421 discovery candidates, 160 engineering checks and 104 workflow scenarios. Discovery reference data comprise 1,263 base fields and 110 broad-topic fields. Initial, accepted and available intermediate reviewer records are retained for the same discovery case identities. Fourteen actual human choices and three unresolved fields remain explicit. Product regression records are retained as auxiliary validation.

## Records and provenance

Discovery records are selected by case identity, independent of answer labels or outcomes. Retained answer objects, confidence values, citations and human choices are unchanged. Original source-record hashes identify the controlled audit files; released-file hashes identify their projected representation. The complete source audit records remain in the authors' local archive. Record projection and byte mappings are provided in `provenance/release_projection.json` and `provenance/export_mapping.json`.

The original section numbers in the base rubric are retained so that existing reviewer citations remain interpretable. Review history reflects symmetric evidence supplements and scoped corrections. Initial agreement and subsequent agreement are calculated separately; human-adjudicated agreement is not represented as model agreement.

Small bibliographic fields, identifiers, structured decisions and source locations are included. Free-form working prose, full abstracts, large author arrays, PDFs, full publisher responses and screenshots remain in the controlled source archive with hash pointers where applicable. Publication aliases replace personal filesystem locations. These aliases identify provenance rather than promising downloadable source files.

## Interpretation

Engineering fixtures measure the specified behaviors in their recorded conditions. Workflow scenarios cover isolated interface, database, file and restart behavior. They are not a count of native mouse-and-keyboard usability trials. All product observations use the same frozen revision; final export validation does not constitute a new product execution.

Discovery proportions describe the screened evaluation panel. Screening decisions and inclusion probabilities remain available. Unresolved-label bounds are identification bounds, not confidence intervals. Inter-model agreement is not model accuracy or a fully human-validated gold standard. Reporting amendments for author attribution and broad topic relevance are documented in the provenance.

## Distribution boundaries

The release excludes research libraries, application databases, credentials, connection tokens, application bundles and third-party full-text documents. Product source is obtained separately under its own access and licensing terms. Access to original literature remains subject to source permissions. No blanket license is granted for third-party materials.

## Current status and historical stages

`protocols/release_definition.json` defines the current scope. Per-case execution adoption, author processing and candidate-level screening are separately mapped to preserved records. Historical stage flags are not current completion flags; their meanings and source relationships are explained in `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`. Superseded draft coverage and sampling manifests remain in the controlled publication-history archive, identified by hashes in `provenance/publication_preparation.json`. This preparation does not rerun the product or alter any reviewer answer.
