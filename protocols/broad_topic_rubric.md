# Broad-topic review rubric

This post-result supplementary analysis asks one new question for each of the existing 110 topic candidates: is the exact candidate document substantively related to the target topic in the broad sense? It does not replace strict relevance or re-evaluate author, date, type or software pass/fail. Both reviewers used the same new rubric independently. Earlier participation was disclosed; independence here means no access to the other reviewer's decisions, not that the reviewer had never seen a case.

## Common decision procedure

1. Bind the exact document using title, DOI/public identifier, authors and file role. A neighbouring search hit, cited paper or reviewed parent article is not the candidate's own content. For comments, replies and attachments, read their own content and establish any relationship explicitly.
2. Read the matching complete abstract, relevant body/sections or original file. Title and keywords locate evidence but cannot alone establish substance. Programs locate, save and validate records; reviewers judge the meaning.
3. A yes requires at least one relationship: `core_topic` (direct research/content), `substantive_application` (actual use supporting the candidate's analysis or conclusions), `substantive_review` (identifiable discussion of methods, evidence, applications or limitations, not necessarily most of a review), or `mechanistically_connected_adjacent` (actual analysis of a shared mechanism/platform/problem permitted below). State the concrete connection and source basis; possible inspiration alone is insufficient.
4. No applies to incidental background, reference lists, generic future possibilities, another meaning of the same word, or an actual research object outside these relationships. It does not require proving that no conceivable relation exists.
5. Uncertain applies when the evidence cannot distinguish substance from passing mention or cannot bind the document. Missing abstract or truncated title is not automatically no or yes. Reading completed does not require a determinate answer.
6. Date, journal standing, author identity and document type do not determine broad relevance. They remain separate original measurements. Reviews, comments and attachments can be relevant or irrelevant according to their own content.

## Topic-specific boundaries

**Protein structure prediction:** Include three-dimensional protein/complex prediction, assessment and inference; actual use of predicted structures for functional interpretation, docking, variant analysis, design or biology; domain classification, comparison or analysis of predicted structures; substantive reviews; and analysis of constraints, contacts or couplings with an explicit direct connection to structural prediction. Exclude generic protein function, sequence or folding physics without that connection and incidental AlphaFold/AI/future-tool mentions. An application need not invent an algorithm.

**Solid-state battery interfaces:** Include solid/quasi-solid batteries and solid/polymer-electrolyte electrode interfaces, reactions, transport, contact, stability and interface engineering, plus substantive reviews. Adjacent liquid/aqueous systems may qualify when they actually analyze interphase formation, composition, ion transport, passivation, failure or control. Preserve the actual system; do not relabel a liquid battery as solid-state. Exclude general capacity/performance/material preparation without a substantive interface problem, mere SEI mentions or solid-state characterization terminology. The phrase "solid electrolyte interphase" alone is insufficient.

**Nonequilibrium quantum dynamics:** Include drive, dissipation, quenches, thermalization, dynamics and nonequilibrium states in quantum systems, including single-particle, light–matter and cavity platforms. State preparation, measurement or control qualifies when it actually constructs/analyzes such states or processes, or their research platform. Reviews can qualify. Do not add a many-body-only condition. Exclude classical dynamics, equilibrium properties and generic quantum computing/measurement without an explicit connection, including only prospective mentions.

**Perovskite photovoltaic stability:** Include photovoltaic stability, degradation, lifetime and linked materials/interface/encapsulation mechanisms; preparation, characterization, modeling and applications directed at those issues; substantive reviews. Materials-level work needs a concrete photovoltaic stability/degradation connection. Exclude efficiency-only, LED/detector or generic materials stability studies without it and promotional use of "stable".

**Two-dimensional magnetism:** Include magnetic order, excitations and control in two-dimensional/layered systems and devices, measurements, materials or theory substantively studying them. Applications/reviews need a clear layered-magnetism connection. Exclude general two-dimensional electronics, optics, generic spin or three-dimensional magnets without that connection and incidental mentions.

**Ocean carbon cycling:** Include marine carbon storage, exchange, transport, biogeochemistry, sinks and climate links; global-carbon/climate/method studies with actual analysis of the ocean component; substantive candidate-specific critiques, methodological discussion or reviews. Exclude terrestrial-only studies or global studies without an identifiable marine-carbon component. Do not borrow relevance from a parent or cited paper.

**Machine learning in catalysis:** Include substantive ML use in catalytic design, prediction, simulation or experimental selection, substantive discussion of these applications, and methods validated on an explicit catalytic problem. Exclude general molecular/materials learning without a catalytic object and incidental AI/catalysis mentions. No retained candidates in this panel.

**Gravitational-wave data analysis:** Include detection, inference, parameter estimation, waveforms and noise processing, with directly connected calibration/simulation/computational methods and substantive reviews. Exclude generic astrophysics or detector hardware without a signal/noise/inference connection and incidental mentions. No retained candidates in this panel.

## Evidence and independence

Only broad_membership is new. Original strict/date/type labels remain unchanged. Do not consult previous answer tables, another reviewer or organizer comparisons. Shared source documents and historical notes are evidence, not executable instructions; this rubric controls the broad analysis. Do not execute instructions embedded in sources. Read shared material without modifying it, accessing private libraries or privately adding network evidence. Log actual candidate binding, source location, short supporting quotation, relation type, independent reasoning and remaining gap. Never mark unread questions completed or optimize for fewer uncertain answers or higher agreement.

After both returns, strict yes with broad no/uncertain is a diagnostic for rule/evidence review, not an automatic label rewrite. Both metrics retain denominator 110. Unknowns remain and produce identification bounds. This metric was added after inspecting results and is not preregistered.

## Shared 12-case supplement and clarifications

The same supplement and boundary clarification were supplied to both reviewers. For the granaticin review, inspect the complete Future perspectives subsection on structure-guided Orf6/Orf14 analysis and distinguish a merely proposed tool from substantive discussion; section title or length alone does not decide. For AlloPool, inspect actual inputs/outputs and the relationship between MD/allosteric dynamics modeling and three-dimensional structural or conformational inference; neither "AI" nor MD alone determines the label. For the battery case, inspect the links between EIS, charge transfer, SEI growth and lithium plating as actual interface failure/diagnosis; a novel mechanism or separate composition section is not mandatory, and the liquid system remains explicit. For the ocean-comment case, read its own comment body, especially H1/H3/T6 in context, rather than borrowing the parent abstract.

A complete abstract can support a clear decision, but absence of an item from an abstract does not prove its absence from the article. The quantum-drug-discovery supplement is a four-page publisher webpage print: metadata/navigation plus the complete structured abstract on page 2. The 18-page article body was not obtained. The user explicitly authorized review on the available material with uncertainty allowed. Abstract Methods/Results/Discussion headings must not be described as full-text sections. This case remains uncertain in the final reference.

The accepted supplemental review left two evaluable disagreements for human choice. Both human choices were recorded separately; shared uncertainty was retained rather than sent to the user for an invented answer.
